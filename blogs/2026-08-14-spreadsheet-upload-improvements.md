Hi everyone, since we launched our spreadsheet geocoding tool last year, it has become one of the primary ways people start using our service. Many thanks to everyone who has taken the time to share feedback with us about how we can make the service even simpler. Based on that feedback and questions we’ve been continually fine-tuning the service and the help pages that explain how things work, especially for less technical users.
